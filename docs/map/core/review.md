Reviews: agent review runs, explanations, groupings by spec, review reports and their specs, the run lifecycle.

Generated by `bun run map`; do not edit. Tests are left out. All folders: [the index](../README.md).

- `review-agent-runs.ts`: Review agents' runs on a review's change: queued by the web, one at a time per change.
- `review-explanations.ts`: The explanations of a review's lines: queued by the web, claimed and finished by the worker.
- `review-groupings.ts`: Groupings of a review's files by spec: queued, claimed, finished and cancelled, one at a time per change.
- `review-report-markdown.ts`: A spec report as markdown, for Copy as markdown on the review page, and its labels.
- `review-report-specs.ts`: The specs a report sends, cleaned (images out of the text, a spec another one holds sent once).
- `review-report-types.ts`: What a spec report holds: the specs it read, its verdict, how the change works and what needs the reviewer.
- `review-reports.ts`: Spec reports of a review: queued, claimed, finished and cancelled, one at a time per change.
- `review-run-lifecycle.ts`: The lifecycle the review side runs share: claim, cancel, fail the interrupted.
- `review-run-rows.ts`: Whether a review side run's row still exists: one removed with its pull request stops its run.
- `review-spec-lists.ts`: What a review's spec list starts with, a stack's specs from its members', the specs as the text Group by spec reads.
- `review-specs.ts`: A review's specs in the database: the list, saving the user's list, and the Notion pages and Jira issues linked to it.
