# Map: `apps/web/client/components/ticket/`

The ticket panel: header and ⋯ menu, its page (hero, status pill, progress, what it needs), tabs and keys, the Overview (details, links, base, size, runs, files, scripts), the review page.

Generated by `bun run map`; do not edit. Tests are left out. All folders: [the index](../README.md).

- `attachments.tsx`: A ticket's files in the browser: one file's row, its note and switch, and the file picker.
- `base-branch-row.tsx`: The branch the ticket starts from and why.
- `branch-picker.tsx`: The repository's branches under a filter box: a ticket's base branch or new target, the branch the Repository tab shows.
- `overview-tab.tsx`: The ticket panel's overview tab: the description beside the details the ticket is attached to, then its runs.
- `parent-picker.tsx`: Select the ticket this one builds on, among the project's other tickets.
- `remove-ticket.tsx`: A cancelled ticket can leave Union; a second click confirms, since nothing brings it back.
- `review-ticket-tab.tsx`: The review page's Ticket tab: the ticket's whole title and its description, in one card.
- `run-table.tsx`: The ticket's runs in the Overview tab: step, model, status and time, with what their agent calls used.
- `script-buttons.tsx`: The project's saved scripts, a chip each that runs it; without any, where to save them.
- `size-row.tsx`: The size of the ticket's change in its details: lines of code added against the plan's estimate, the files on hover.
- `status-pill.tsx`: A ticket's state as a pill with a dot, the same on the board's cards and in the panel; a live state's dot pulses.
- `ticket-attachments.tsx`: The ticket's files, in its Overview tab: added, described and removed at any time.
- `ticket-attention.tsx`: The card under the ticket's progress saying what it needs from the user now, with the button that does it.
- `ticket-description.tsx`: The ticket's description, folded when long, and its title and description edited in place.
- `ticket-details.tsx`: What the ticket is attached to: where it comes from, what it depends on, its size, its files, its preview.
- `ticket-hero.tsx`: The top of the ticket panel's page: its title, its state, flow and branch, its progress and what it needs.
- `ticket-links.tsx`: "Depends on" (editable until the ticket starts) and the tickets that build on this one.
- `ticket-menu.tsx`: The header's ⋯ menu: the actions that end a ticket, kept out of the way: Cancel ticket, then Remove ticket.
- `ticket-panel-header.tsx`: The ticket panel's top bar: where the ticket lives (repository, issue, pull request) and its actions.
- `ticket-panel-keys.ts`: The ticket panel's keys: Escape closes it, r opens the review, 1 to 5 switch its tabs.
- `ticket-panel-page.tsx`: The ticket panel's scrolling page: its hero (title, progress, what it needs), the tabs, the open tab's body.
- `ticket-panel.tsx`: The side panel for the selected ticket: its top bar, then one scroll: title, progress, tabs, the tab's body.
- `ticket-progress.tsx`: The ticket's way through its flow, drawn as stops: each step's state and what it did; a click opens its tab.
- `ticket-review.tsx`: A ticket's review page: its diff beside tabs for its description, preview, pull request, spec report and review agents.
- `ticket-tabs.tsx`: The panel's top tabs: the ticket, its preview, the agents' work, its pull request and the tracker item's comments.
- `ticket-text-editor.tsx`: The ticket's title and description being edited on its Overview, saved to the issue by the worker.
- `use-panel-tabs.ts`: The ticket panel's open tab and Worker sub-tab, following the ticket to its current step; opening a step's.
