# Map: `apps/web/client/components/jira/`

A ticket's Jira tab: its comments and replies.

Generated by `bun run map`; do not edit. Tests are left out. All folders: [the index](../README.md).

- `jira-comment-list.tsx`: A ticket's Jira comments in its Jira tab, each with Add to review and Reply, then New comment.
- `jira-comment.tsx`: One comment of a ticket's Jira issue: who wrote it and when, who may see it, and its text.
- `jira-issue-card.tsx`: The Jira tab's issue card: its summary, key, type, status, assignee and last update, Open in Jira, Refresh.
- `jira-property-input.tsx`: The new value of a Jira issue's field in its tab: the rule editor's input without variables, or a move for the status.
- `jira-reply.tsx`: A new comment on a ticket's Jira issue, or a reply to one (it mentions the author).
- `jira-status-input.tsx`: Moving a Jira issue in its tab: the transitions its workflow offers from where it is, read when the editor opens.
- `jira-tab.tsx`: A ticket's Jira tab: the issue's card, then its comments, read once per opening.
