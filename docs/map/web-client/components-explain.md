# Map: `apps/web/client/components/explain/`

A review page's Explain tab: its conversations, their turns and answers, asking, the Explain state the diff shares.

Generated by `bun run map`; do not edit. Tests are left out. All folders: [the index](../README.md).

- `conversation-header.tsx`: The top of an open Explain conversation: its title (renamed in place), how many questions, Export and Delete.
- `conversation-list.tsx`: The Explain conversations beside the open one: New, a search, then each with its state, question count and date.
- `explain-composer.tsx`: The box under an Explain conversation (or a new one): a question for the agent, sent with Ask or Ctrl+Enter.
- `explain-context.tsx`: The review page's Explain state: asking (which opens the Explain tab on its conversation), the specs, the lines to show.
- `explain-export.ts`: An Explain conversation as a Markdown file: its title, then each question, its subjects and its answer, oldest first.
- `explain-section.tsx`: The Explain tab of a review page: after the Report tab (else last before the diff), counted while the agent answers.
- `explain-tab.tsx`: The review page's Explain tab: the conversations on the left, the open one (or a new one) on the right.
- `explain-turn.tsx`: One turn of an Explain conversation: the question and what it is about, then the answer, its progress or its error.
- `explanation-answer.tsx`: An explanation's answer: how the lines work with their cited code, the specs and docs they relate to.
- `explanation-progress.tsx`: While an explanation waits or runs: its status, the agent's last steps (the files it reads) and Cancel.
- `open-conversation.ts`: The Explain conversation the review page shows, kept in the URL (`?explain=<id>`, or `new` for a new one).
